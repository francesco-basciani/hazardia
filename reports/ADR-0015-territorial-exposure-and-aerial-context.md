# ADR-0015 — Territorial exposure read model and unqualified aerial capability

Status: accepted implementation of the19October2026 user request. AR-EXT-01 and original M0–M7 gates preserved.

Use optional geospatial read models beside the original immutable physical results. A bounded worker consumes the verified native probability/arrival/fire arrays and a sourced territorial inventory; the UI binds each map to its own result and the shared replay clock. Failure of the inventory does not modify physical outputs or imply low risk. GeoNames locality points and WorldCover built-up polygons have different meanings; neither yields population or vulnerability. Mean pixel probability and frequency of any contact in stochastic realizations are separate metrics. Earliest/median contact is conditional on simulated contact. Source buffers75m/100m are declared research assumptions.

Prefer bounded OSM acquisition, retain unsuccessful attempts, reuse licensed cached extracts and WorldCover/GeoNames with explicit missing coverage. Support immutable, bounded local OSM JSON imports for reproducibility and geographic portability. Do not require commercial services or bulk national downloads. Cache geometry3h, missing live source5min; limit external OSM requests10/day, response2MiB (official small extract4MiB), region1°×1°, workers2 and geospatial subprocess100s. Native context results identify their original job and remain frozen until explicit refresh. No population estimates or official alerts are produced by this read model.

Render known overhead lines, minor lines, explicitly overhead cables and supports. Underground/underwater cables are excluded; unknown voltage/height remain unknown. Cartographic intervention intersection is not a flight-path or clearance assessment. Approximate solar elevation describes day/twilight/night at the scenario time; it is not an authorization criterion.

The existing solver/independent verifier retains its contracts. Unqualified aerial resources are conservatively unavailable to automatic assignment; reject aerial resources again at native replay, including old decisions. Ground research planning can continue. Manual physical experiments remain possible with an explicit nonqualification warning. No routes, conductor sag, aircraft/crew qualifications or operational flight authority are inferred from OSM.

Alternatives rejected: manufacture electrical corridors from satellite imagery; infer wire height from voltage; equate empty OSM queries with obstacle-free airspace; publish a calibrated human-risk map from built-up class; shrink old fire imagery to imply protection. Full certified obstacle data, population/vulnerability and operational aerial logistics require separate qualification. Evidence and negative attempts: `ABITATI-ESPOSIZIONE-E-LINEE-ELETTRICHE.md` and `territorial-exposure/verification.json`.
